# 527 West 27th Street — rundown (internal)

Pulled 2026-10-14. Seventh PJ site. Offer came by email to PJ from **Issayas Tesfai, Centaur Properties** (the developer/owner), with an SBLM floor plan. Both in `listing/`.

## TL;DR

- **The space:** ground floor of **Jardim**, the Isay Weinfeld condo at 527 W 27th. **2,410 sf ground + 2,120 sf cellar**, elevator, 12–13 ft ceilings. Shape is **wide and shallow: ~61 ft 7 in of 27th Street frontage, 33 ft 4 in deep**, with the elevator/stair core at the east end. White box, never occupied.
- **It is itself a dark door.** Owner's own city filings say the retail was **vacant on 31 Dec 2023, 2024 and 2025**. Building finished 2019. Listed on and off since (Kassin Sabbagh, then Newmark's Ravi Idnani, listing refreshed 18 May 2026, "immediate", price on request). Dec 2024 permit added **four restrooms to split the ground floor into four units** — so they are prepared to cut it up.
- **The block is in transition, not in business.** Old Club Row. North side: 515 (Bungalow 8) **to be demolished for a 16-storey tower**; 533 (Scores) **sold, full-demolition filing Sept 2025, 12-storey rentals planned**; 509/519/521 **for sale as a portfolio**. South side: 530 (McKittrick / Sleep No More) **closed Jan 2025, nothing announced**; 518 (Hotel Americano → Selina) **Yelp says closed**; 520's retail shutters down in the April 2026 Street View.
- **Foot traffic comes from one thing: the High Line stairs at 28th and 26th**, plus the galleries (547 W 27th has ~25 galleries; High Line Nine at 507 W 27th). Nearest subway is **34 St–Hudson Yards (7), 543 m**; 23 St (C/E) 733 m. Nobody counts the doors on this block; the city's own numbers are in `data/` once the pull finishes.
- **Verdict for the build:** a very good *room* (61 ft of glass, sliding panels, elevator, cellar) on a *quiet* street that will be a construction zone on both sides of the door for 2–3 years. Rent unknown; ask.

## The offer

- From: Issayas Tesfai, Centaur Properties (530 W 28th St office). "A few brand new white box retail spaces on W 27th and W 28th, just west of the High Line." 527 W 27th: "2,400+ sf above grade, elevator, tall ceilings." Offered a tour.
- Plan (SBLM Architects, "CENTAUR — West 27th Street Space", 3/32" = 1'): **Ground 2,410 sf**, 61'-7" along 27th St × 33'-4" deep; elevator 5'-9" × 8'-8", stair 22'-6" × 8'-0" at the east end; a door at the far west end of the street wall. **Cellar 2,120 sf**, 46'-0" × 33'-0" plus the core.
- Online listing (PropertyShark / LoopNet 40302366, updated 18 May 2026, broker **Ravi Idnani, Newmark**): Ground 2,410 sf + Lower 2,210 sf = 4,620 sf, divisible, immediate, price on request. Copy: "no use restrictions", "parking spots can be made available", "27th Street unit has a private elevator and a roof where sculptures can be displayed", "**storefront window panels slide open completely on 27th Street**", ideal uses wine bar / restaurant / cafe / bakery. Earlier (Sept 2025) KSR had it as 4 spaces totalling 9,329 sf across 527 W 27th + 532 W 28th, with the 28th St unit at 78 ft frontage.
- **Open:** rent, term, who pays the fit-out, whether the four-way split is already built (restroom permit M01150080, Dec 2024, "LOC issued").

## The building

- **Jardim**, 527 W 27th (south building) + 528/532 W 28th (Jardim Norte). BBL 1-699-7504 (condo), BIN 1090205. Lat/lon 40.751109, -74.003437. Lot 100 ft × 197.5 ft, through-block.
- Isay Weinfeld's first NYC building; developers **Centaur Properties + Greyscale Development**; builder Pizzarotti. Topped out 2017, finished 2019 (PLUTO says 2015). 11 storeys, **36 condos**, 2 commercial units, PLUTO retail area 9,557 sf. Zoning **C6-3, Special West Chelsea District**, no historic district. Condo units trade $3M–$18M (PHA sold $17.995M June 2024).
- Site history: Crobar → Pink Elephant → Mansion nightclub (530 W 28th), bought by Centaur 2013/14 for $45M.
- Facade (PJ's photo + Street View Apr 2026, `listing/streetview/`), west to east: corten door, **the retail glass** (three panes, lease sign), concrete with the brass "Jardim" script, the **through-block tunnel**, the "527" pier. **The curved stainless storefront with two rounded bays east of the pier is the neighbour at 519–521 W 27th, not Jardim** (I had that wrong on the first pass). Across the street: dark grey grid facade with white roll-down shutters, down.
- Open DOB items: sidewalk-shed permit **15 Apr 2026** (water-infiltration repairs, approved Apr 2024) — expect a shed on the frontage; 2026 complaints at 527 (Apr 8 active, Apr 10 ×2, Sept 26 closed). 311: two parking complaints in 2026. No HPD, no liquor licence, no restaurant inspection history at the address ever.

## Street feel (Street View Apr 2026 + records)

- Wide one-way street, parked cars both sides, box trucks, **594 street trees within the pull radius** but few on this block face. Mid-rise glass-and-concrete new builds on the north side, old brick lofts/garages across. Sidewalk sheds likely at 527 (permit Apr 2026), 515 and 533 (demolition).
- Daytime: gallery-goers and Avenues parents/students, High Line overflow coming down the 28th St stairs. Night: Marquee at the 10th Ave corner, otherwise dark.
- Identity cues for the page, from the place's physics: the High Line steel (black/rust green), the Jardim concrete + brass script, the 7-train purple, the stainless curve of the window.

## Risks / what to ask Centaur

1. Rent and term; whether the ground floor is still one 2,410 sf room or already split four ways.
2. Construction next door (515) and two doors west (533): dates, shed, noise. Both are multi-year.
3. Is the 28th St unit (532 W 28th, 78 ft frontage) also on offer, and what is the price gap.
4. Whether the corten door at the west end is part of the unit (service / elevator lobby) or the building's.
5. Condo board rules on signage, hours, music, window vinyl.
