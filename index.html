527 West 27th Street: the retail floor of Jardim, Isay Weinfeld's concrete condominium between Tenth and Eleventh Avenues, a half block west of the High Line. 61 feet of frontage, 33 feet deep, 2,410 square feet and a cellar, with an elevator. Finished in 2019. Never let. Every building here is real, at its recorded height; the green ribbon is the High Line.

The building

Jardim / 527 West 27th

Two buildings on one lot that runs through from 27th to 28th, with a garden between them and a car tunnel under it. Isay Weinfeld's first building in New York. Centaur Properties and Greyscale bought the site in 2014 for $45 million; the Crobar and Pink Elephant nightclubs stood here. Topped out 2017, finished 2019.

The space
2,410sq ft on the ground, from the architect's plan. 2,120 more in the cellar.

Wide and shallow

A room 61 feet 7 inches along the street and 33 feet 4 inches deep, with the elevator and a stair at one end. About 32 feet of it is glass, three panes in a dark bronze frame, with a "retail space for lease" sign in the first pane. White box. Ceilings 12 to 13 feet. The storefront panels are said to slide open.

Four units?
A December 2024 permit added four restrooms "on the 1st floor retail spaces". The owner is ready to cut it up.
Listed
Newmark, refreshed May 2026: 2,410 ground + 2,210 lower, divisible, immediate, price on request. Before that Kassin Sabbagh, as four spaces totalling 9,329 sq ft with the 28th Street unit.
Empty since
The owner's own city filings: vacant on 31 December 2023, 2024 and 2025. No business has ever been licensed, inspected or permitted at the address.

The street

22 percent dark

Within 250 m, in the 2024 reporting year, 15 of 67 storefronts were filed vacant on December 31. The Comptroller's June 2026 report has the city at 11.0 percent. Most lots on this block have never filed at all.

Dark doors

Club Row, after the clubs

Twenty years ago this block was Bungalow 8, Cain, Home, Guest House, Spirit, B.E.D., Pink Elephant and Scores, ten thousand people a night. Today the only one open is Marquee on the corner. The rest are waiting to be demolished.
